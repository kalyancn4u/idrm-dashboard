What it does

IDRM inventories identity access, evaluates it against a catalogue of risk rules, and records every change in an immutable audit trail.

    Identities
    People, contractors, vendors, service accounts and robots
    Accounts
    Their logins on governed systems
    Grants
    Entitlements assigned to those accounts
    Findings
    Risk-rule violations with SLA, owner and evidence
    Audit
    Append-only, tamper-evident record of every change

Two modes

Admin — remediate, revoke, certify, scan.

Auditor — read, filter, export. No writes, structurally.
